## Why users hold ITP

- auto-compounder reward systems

## Staking and reward context

The broader stack includes staking and reward-tracking workflows (including epoch-based yield analytics in internal tooling), plus LP reward systems where users can receive ITP-aligned incentives.

In newer boosted LP patterns, reward delivery can include:

- ITP reward flow when available
- fallback behavior in paired reward assets when configured
- user protection mechanics such as cooldown periods

## Practical user mindset

- ITP is strongest when used inside product flows (vaults, LP, automation), not treated as an isolated ticker.
- The core thesis is usage-driven utility: more real product activity can support a stronger long-term token narrative.

## Important note
