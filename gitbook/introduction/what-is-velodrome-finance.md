### What is Optimism Superchain? <a href="#id-3" id="id-3"></a>

To fully appreciate Velodrome Finance, it’s essential to understand what the Optimism Superchain is. As a network of interconnected Layer-2 solutions sharing a common development stack, the Optimism Superchain is a scalable and secure ecosystem designed to enhance the Ethereum network. By employing optimistic rollups, Optimism significantly reduces gas fees and increases transaction throughput. This underlying infrastructure provides the foundation for high-performance DeFi protocols like Velodrome Finance to thrive.

#### How Optimism Superchain benefits Velodrome Finance

Compared to other Layer-2 solutions like Arbitrum and Polygon, Optimism stands out due to its developer-friendly tools, robust security measures, and seamless integration with the Ethereum ecosystem. By harnessing the power of Optimism, Velodrome Finance experiences notably lower transaction costs, faster trade execution, and a smoother overall user experience. This combination of factors has been instrumental in attracting a growing community of traders and liquidity providers to the platform.

### A deep dive into how Velodrome Finance works <a href="#id-4" id="id-4"></a>

Now that we’re familiar with the foundations Velodrome Finance is built upon, let's explore the mechanics of how Velodrome Finance works and understand what its key features are. At its core, Velodrome Finance functions as an AMM, and facilitates token swaps through liquidity pools. However, it goes beyond basic swapping by introducing a suite of innovative features designed to maximize returns for both traders and liquidity providers.

#### Gauge weights

A cornerstone of Velodrome Finance is the gauge weight system, which empowers users to directly influence the platform's trajectory and democratizes liquidity distribution. By staking the platform's native token, VELO, users can allocate voting power to determine how liquidity is distributed across different pools. This democratic approach makes sure the platform's resources are aligned with the community's preferences, because those who are staking VELO have the right to decide where the liquidity should be allocated.

For instance, if a community is particularly interested in a specific trading pair, they can allocate more voting power to that pool's gauge, incentivizing liquidity providers to add to that pool and ultimately improving the trading experience for everyone involved.

#### veVELO token

Previously known as ve(3,3) tokens, the veVELO token is an integral part of Velodrome Finance, amplifying the impact of governance and rewarding long-term commitment. By locking up VELO tokens for varying durations (typically 3, 6, or 9 months), users create veVELO tokens, which grant enhanced voting power and increased rewards.

To illustrate this better, a user who locks up VELO for nine months will receive a higher veVELO balance compared to someone who locks for three months. This means they’ll have a stronger influence on governance decisions and earn more rewards. This mechanism incentivizes users to strategically lock their VELO tokens, aligning their interests with the long-term health of the protocol.

#### Liquidity mining

Velodrome Finance offers a robust liquidity mining program to attract and retain liquidity providers beyond a share of trading fees. By depositing tokens into liquidity pools, users can unlock a multiplier effect on their gains in the form of VELO. This incentivized approach helps to ensure deep liquidity for swappers and tighter spreads, benefiting all participants in the ecosystem.

Here’s an in-depth look at Velodrome Finance’s liquidity mining system:

* Earn trading fees: As a liquidity provider, you’ll earn a portion of the trading fees generated within the pool you contribute to.
* VELO rewards: Velodrome Finance allocates a portion of its platform fees to be distributed as VELO rewards to liquidity providers. The amount of VELO received is proportional to the user’s contribution to the pool’s liquidity.
* Boosted VELO rewards with veVELO: By locking VELO tokens, users can create veVELO tokens, and significantly amplify their VELO rewards from liquidity mining. The longer the lock duration, the greater the boost in rewards.

This combined approach allows users to benefit from contributing liquidity and gain influence over the platform’s governance. By providing deep liquidity, liquidity providers enable efficient and low-slippage trades for users, while traders generate the fees that contribute to liquidity provider rewards. Ultimately, this symbiotic relationship between traders and liquidity providers benefits the entire Velodrome ecosystem.

By combining these elements, Velodrome Finance creates a unique user experience by empowering the community, incentivizing long-term engagement, and rewarding contributions to the platform’s liquidity. This three-pronged approach sets Velodrome apart from other AMMs and is a core reason for the AMM’s success.

### Velodrome V2: what's new <a href="#id-5" id="id-5"></a>

In December 2022, Velodrome announced [Velodrome V2](https://medium.com/@VelodromeFi/velodrome-v2-a-new-era-1bd84509fa23?source=user_profile---------8----------------------------), which represented a significant leap forward in decentralized exchange technology. Some noteworthy features include concentrated liquidity pools (clAMMs), customizable fees, and dynamic emissions rates with [VELO FED](https://medium.com/@VelodromeFi/velodrome-the-infinite-race-ecac269cc302). These features ultimately empower both traders and liquidity providers to optimize their strategies.

Customizable fees offer traders unprecedented flexibility. Regardless of whether they’re seeking the lowest possible slippage or prioritizing guaranteed trade execution, users can tailor fee structures to suit their specific needs. This dynamic approach to fee determination fosters a more competitive and efficient trading environment.

To further enhance the user experience, Velodrome V2 introduces VELO FED. As the name suggests, VELO FED is an onchain treasury designed to support the ecosystem's growth. It will give veVELO voters the power to set an overarching monetary policy for VELO supply and determine VELO emission rates. By strategically managing platform fees and rewards, VELO FED plays a crucial role in stabilizing the platform and providing additional incentives for liquidity providers and traders.

clAMMs revolutionize liquidity management by allowing liquidity providers to focus their capital within specific price ranges, ultimately enhancing capital efficiency and increasing potential returns. This innovation benefits liquidity providers and improves the overall trading experience by reducing slippage and ensuring deeper liquidity in high-demand price areas.

Together, these features position Velodrome V2 as a leading-edge platform that caters to the evolving needs of the DeFi ecosystem.

### Velodrome vs Aerodrome <a href="#id-7" id="id-7"></a>

Velodrome or Aerodrome? This is a common question that comes up with Velodrome Finance being mentioned. Wondering how it differs from [Aerodrome Finance](https://aerodrome.finance/)? Let's now look at the similarities and differences between both DeFi protocols.

For starters, both DApps share a strong foundation, being built on the Solidly fork and operating on Layer-2 solutions. Their core mechanics are quite similar as both platforms introduce gauge voting, ve-style tokenomics, and liquidity incentives. However, they differentiate themselves based on the underlying blockchain, ecosystem focus, and specific features.

* Blockchain: Velodrome Finance operates on the Optimism network, while Aerodrome Finance is deployed on Base.
* Ecosystem focus: Velodrome Finance has established itself as a cornerstone of the Optimism ecosystem, while Aerodrome Finance aims to become the central liquidity hub for Base.
* User base: Velodrome Finance generally has a larger and more established user base due to its earlier launch and the maturity of the Optimism network.
* Pool creation: Velodrome Finance offers permissionless pool creation, allowing users to create new liquidity pools, while Aerodrome Finance's pool creation process might be more curated.

#### Key considerations for users

When choosing between Velodrome and Aerodrome, users should consider the following factors:

* Blockchain preference: If you’re invested in the Optimism ecosystem, Velodrome Finance might be a natural choice. If you’re interested in Base, Aerodrome Finance is the obvious option.
* Fee structure: Compare the fee structures of both platforms to determine which offers better value for your trading volume.
* Liquidity: Evaluate the depth of liquidity available for your desired trading pairs on both platforms.
* Governance model: Consider the governance mechanisms and tokenomics of each platform to assess your level of influence and potential rewards.
* Risk tolerance: Aerodrome Finance, as a newer platform, might carry higher risks but also potentially offer greater rewards.
